Strict Moderation Armor

The 7 Universal Compliance Rules

Every prompt compiled across Module 1 and Module 3 is automatically filtered through seven hard commercial gates to prevent marketplace policy violations.

RULE 01

Universal Zero-Female Protocol

Strict automated suppression of female figures across human, cartoon, robot, and mascot formats. Focuses on commercial still life, nature, architecture, and technology.

RULE 02

Zero Trademark & Trade Dress

Automatic removal of brand names, iconic phone contours, vehicle emblems, and patent-protected industrial styling to guarantee commercial clearance.

RULE 03

Strict Faceless Clause

When people are requested, the engine mandates over-the-shoulder, silhouette, or hands-only angles to eliminate the need for model releases.

RULE 04

Copy Space Reservation

Ensures intentional 30%–40% isolated negative space for banner ads, book covers, and marketing headlines, which is the top preference of commercial buyers.

RULE 05

Knolling Contour Grid

For sticker sheets and icon collections, prompt parameters strictly enforce symmetrical die-cut white cutlines without cut-off borders.

RULE 06

Optical Physics Precision

Enforces physically accurate camera lenses (such as 85mm prime or 100mm macro) and softbox lighting setups rather than vague decorative adjectives.

RULE 07

Physical Micro-Textures & Depth of Field

Enforces genuine surface micro-textures (wood grain, tactile linen, clean glass refraction) and optical lens apertures from f/1.8 to f/2.8 to eradicate the artificial plastic sheen of generic AI imagery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Microstock contributor queries answered

Does this require uploading my high-resolution images to third-party servers? ↓

No. The upscaling engine (Real-ESRGAN Vulkan NCNN) runs locally on your computer GPU without internet access. Only the lightweight visual inspection and metadata prompt queries are routed through Google Gemini Vision.

Why exactly 50 keywords? ↓

Adobe Stock and Shutterstock permit up to 50 keywords. Having exactly 50 highly relevant, sanitized keywords maximizes marketplace search indexing while avoiding algorithmic spam penalties.

How does the Downloads folder auto-sync handle active browser downloads? ↓

The scanner detects incomplete .crdownload files and pauses processing on those specific items until Chrome or Safari completes writing the final image to disk.
